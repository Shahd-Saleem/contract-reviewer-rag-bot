# Commercial Real Estate Lease Agreement

## 1. Term and Rent
This lease agreement begins on October 1, 2026, and expires on September 30, 2029. The tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $4,500 due on the 1st day of each month. A late fee of 8% of the total monthly rent will be assessed automatically if payment is received after 11:59 PM on the 5th day of the month.

## 2. Maintenance and Repair Responsibilities
The Tenant is solely responsible for maintaining HVAC units, plumbing systems, and electrical fixtures within the demised premises. Repairs exceeding $1,500 must be approved in writing by the Landlord, but costs remain the Tenant's responsibility. The Landlord is responsible solely for structural foundation and exterior roof repairs.

## 3. Early Termination Penalty
Tenant may not terminate this Agreement prior to the expiration date without written consent. Early termination by the Tenant incurs a mandatory buyout fee equal to 4 months of base rent ($18,000) plus forfeiture of the initial security deposit ($9,000). Written notice must be provided 120 days prior to departure.

## 4. Default and Eviction Notice
In the event of non-payment or breach of terms, the Landlord shall provide a 5-day written notice to cure default before filing summary eviction proceedings.
