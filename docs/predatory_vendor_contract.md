# Software Development & Maintenance Agreement

## 1. Services and Payment Terms
Client agrees to pay Vendor a baseline monthly fee of $2,500 due on the 1st of each month. 

## 2. Automatic Price Escalation Clause
The baseline monthly fee is subject to an automatic 15% increase every 6 months without prior notice or consent required from the Client.

## 3. Unilateral Intellectual Property Assignment
All code, designs, algorithms, and intellectual property developed under this Agreement, including proprietary concepts provided directly by the Client, shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Vendor. Client receives a non-transferable, revocable operational license only.

## 4. Mandatory Auto-Renewal and Extreme Cancellation Notice
This Agreement automatically renews for 2-year terms. To cancel, Client must provide written notice via certified physical mail between exactly 90 and 120 days prior to the annual renewal date. Failure to meet this precise window forfeits the right to terminate and locks the Client into the full 2-year renewal cost.

## 5. Uncapped Exclusivity and Restrictive Covenant
Client is strictly prohibited from hiring any other third-party software developer or maintaining an in-house engineering team for 24 months following contract termination. Breach of this covenant results in a mandatory liquidated damages fee of $100,000.

## 6. Liability Shift and Subcontracting
Vendor disclaims all warranties regarding software performance and uptime. Vendor reserves the right to offshore all development work to unvetted third parties without notifying Client. Client agrees to indemnify Vendor against all third-party legal claims arising from software failures.
