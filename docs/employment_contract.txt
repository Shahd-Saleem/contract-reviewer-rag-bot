EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee is hired as a Senior Software Engineer. Duties include designing, testing, and maintaining core backend services as directed by management.

2. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
Employee shall receive a base salary of $110,000 per annum, paid in semi-monthly installments. Employee is eligible for standard medical insurance and 15 business days of paid time off (PTO) per year.

3. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
For a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not directly or indirectly engage in, or assist any business that competes with Employer within a 50-mile radius of Employer's primary office location.

4. SEVERANCE PAY
If Employee is terminated without cause, Employer shall provide severance pay equivalent to two (2) weeks of base salary for every full year of service, provided Employee executes a general release of claims.
