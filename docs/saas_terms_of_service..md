# Enterprise Cloud SaaS Terms of Service

## 1. Subscription Renewal and Cancellation Notice
All enterprise software subscriptions auto-renew annually on the anniversary date. To prevent auto-renewal, the Customer must submit a formal cancellation notice via registered mail or verified account dashboard at least 60 days prior to the renewal date. Cancellations submitted via email or regular mail will not be honored.

## 2. Data Retention and Export upon Termination
Upon contract termination or expiration, Customer data will be retained for 30 business days. After 30 days, all stored customer records, logs, and database backups will be permanently purged from cloud storage servers. The Customer must request data export services in writing prior to termination; export service fees start at $2,500.

## 3. Limitation of Liability and SLA Credits
Provider uptime commitment is 99.5% per calendar month. If service availability falls below 99.5%, Customer is eligible for service credits capped at a maximum of 15% of the monthly billing cycle. In no event shall Provider's total aggregate liability exceed the total amounts paid by Customer in the preceding 3-month period.
